# Weierstrass group-scheme dependency audit

Date: 2026-08-03

This audit records the checked dependency boundary for constructing
`WeierstrassGroupSchemeInterface` in
`MazurTorsion.ModularCurve.XZeroFiniteFlatModuli`. It makes no roadmap-node
completion claim.

## Exact dependency revisions

- Mathlib: `79d0395a1825a6264ad5d269e35e60537518955e`
  (`v4.33.0-rc1`).
- Tau Ceti: `a74dfee78f800df63f085a19006f7d502eee365e`.
- AINTLIB material: the attributed ports under
  `MazurTorsion/Upstream/AINTLIB/` at this repository revision; it is not a
  separately linked Lake package.

The revisions above are read directly from the root `lake-manifest.json`.

## Reproducible source search

From the repository root, the relevant negative and positive searches are:

```text
rg -n -i 'projective.*(weierstrass|elliptic)|weierstrass.*(scheme|projective)|elliptic.*group.*scheme|group.*scheme.*elliptic|rational.*point.*equiv' \
  .lake/packages/mathlib/Mathlib .lake/packages/TauCeti/TauCeti \
  EllipticCurves MazurTorsion --glob '*.lean'

rg -n -i 'elliptic' .lake/packages/TauCeti/TauCeti --glob '*.lean'

rg -n 'AbelianVariety|CommGroupScheme|FiniteFlatCommGroupScheme' \
  .lake/packages/TauCeti/TauCeti MazurTorsion/Upstream/AINTLIB \
  MazurTorsion --glob '*.lean'
```

The audit found the following.

1. Mathlib's files under
   `Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/Projective/` construct
   homogeneous coordinate triples, their quotient by scaling, the
   nonsingular-point type, and its group law. The word `Projective` in this
   API does not denote an `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme`, and there is no
   projective Weierstrass group scheme or comparison with scheme-valued
   points.
2. The imported AINTLIB ports construct finite-flat commutative group-scheme
   infrastructure and the Picard group of a scheme. They do not construct
   the projective Weierstrass cubic or its group-scheme law.
3. Tau Ceti does contain an actual
   `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety`. Its `toOver` field is a group
   object over `Spec K`; properness and geometric integrality imply
   commutativity by Tau Ceti's checked rigidity theorem. The pinned tree has
   no constructor from a Mathlib `WeierstrassCurve`, and its mention of
   `JacobianVariety` is prospective API documentation rather than a checked
   declaration.

## Checked reduction now available

`MazurTorsion.ModularCurve.XZeroWeierstrassAbelianVariety` introduces
`WeierstrassAbelianVarietyComparison`. It packages a Tau Ceti abelian variety
and only the rational-point multiplicative equivalence. The checked
constructor `toGroupSchemeInterface` obtains the required commutative group
scheme from the abelian variety. Its real downstream consumer
`WeierstrassAbelianVarietyComparison.splitGammaZeroDatumOfTorsion` constructs
the finite-flat split cyclic subgroup used by the `X₀(N)` lane, and
`splitGammaZeroDatumPointHom_range` verifies that its rational points recover
the coordinate subgroup generated by the marked torsion point.

The constructor `ofProjectivePointEquiv` further uses Mathlib's checked
`WeierstrassCurve.Projective.Point.toAffineAddEquiv`. Consequently the future
scheme model may prove the natural comparison with homogeneous projective
coordinate points; no separate affine-coordinate group comparison is then
needed. `splitGammaZeroDatumOfProjectivePointComparison` is a compiled
downstream consumer of precisely that boundary.

This removes internal-group and commutativity packaging from the remaining
Weierstrass task. It does not assume representability or identify coordinate
points by fiat.

## Remaining mathematical boundary

The full `MT-X0-MODULI` acceptance boundary still requires all of the
following checked constructions for every elliptic Weierstrass curve over
the relevant field:

1. an actual projective cubic scheme over `Spec K` (for example from the
   homogeneous cubic and a checked `Proj`/closed-subscheme construction);
2. its multiplication, identity, and inverse as scheme morphisms, with the
   group laws, plus the properness and geometric-integrality facts needed to
   package it as Tau Ceti's `AbelianVariety` (or an equivalent direct
   commutative group-scheme construction);
3. a multiplicative equivalence between morphisms `Spec K ⟶ E` and
   Mathlib's projective coordinate-point group
   `Multiplicative W.toProjective.Point`; the new bridge then derives the
   affine-coordinate comparison.

No searched dependency supplies these declarations at the pinned revisions.
They remain formalization work rather than external blockers.
